The design must follow a premium, minimal, editorial approach.

I want the website to feel like the portfolio of an experienced AI and infrastructure engineer who builds serious products.

### Visual direction

Use:

- Generous whitespace.
- Strong typography.
- Sophisticated visual hierarchy.
- Carefully selected colors.
- Beautiful project imagery.
- Subtle motion.
- Elegant transitions.
- Refined interactive elements.

### Avoid

Do not use:
- Excessive gradients.
- Neon colors.
- Overused purple AI gradients.
- Matrix-style backgrounds.
- Excessive glassmorphism.
- Unnecessary floating elements.
- Random animated particles.
- Excessive glowing effects.
- Generic AI-generated developer illustrations.
- Huge numbers of technology icons.
- Overloaded dashboards.
- Unnecessary animations that distract from the content.

Do not make the website look like a cyberpunk interface, a gaming website, or a typical template generated by an AI website builder.

### Suggested design system

Use a refined light theme as the primary experience.

Possible palette:
- Background: warm white or subtle off-white.
- Primary text: deep charcoal.
- Secondary text: muted gray.
- Accent: sophisticated blue, indigo, or muted teal.
- Borders: subtle neutral gray.
- Cards: white with understated borders.

A dark theme may be supported as an optional enhancement.

Typography should feel premium and editorial. Use a modern sans-serif font such as Geist, Inter, or a similarly refined alternative.

The design must remain consistent across every page.
